                                    * Running time:2h 6m
                                    * Release Date: 1 April 2021
                                    * Genre: Mystery Drama
                                    * Language: Malayalam
                                    * Country: India
                                    * Plot: Set during the 2020 COVID-19 lockdown, the film follows Roy and Shirley, a couple who travel from Mumbai to Kerala to visit Shirley's father, Ittyavira. As they navigate personal and financial challenges, Ittyavira reveals a long-held secret that forces them to confront moral dilemmas and the complexities of familial bonds.
